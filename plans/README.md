## Findings considered and rejected

- KeyboardKit as a dependency: versions 10+ are closed source with binary frameworks; 9.9.x
  is MIT but stale and SwiftUI-gesture based.
- FUTO Keyboard's code, transformer and swipe weights: licences forbid commercial use or add
  restrictions incompatible with GPL-3.0-only.
- Reading the whole document by moving the cursor (`adjustTextPosition` paging) for
  correction capture: visible caret jumps and unreliable on hosts; anchor tracking instead.
- Bouncing to the app to record: Murmur already records inside the keyboard through WebKit,
  and guideline 4.4.1 bars keyboards from launching other apps.
- `UITextChecker` as the main autocorrect: unscored one-edit guesses with no context; kept as
  a fallback only.
